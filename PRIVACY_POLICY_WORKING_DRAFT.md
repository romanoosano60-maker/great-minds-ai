## What the product is
Great Minds AI is a developing educational product for children and their parents/guardians and teachers. The current public project includes learning pages and demonstration features. Some prototype features save progress only in the browser on the device. Cloud-backed account, classroom and learning-progress features are still undergoing integration and verification.

## Data handling — must be confirmed before release
The owner and technical team must document, for the exact release build:
- Account identifiers and email addresses handled by the authentication provider.
- Student profile fields and whether nicknames or any direct identifiers are stored.
- Parent/guardian relationship and consent records.
- Classroom membership, teacher approval and classroom activity.
- Quiz scores, homework submissions, learning progress and reward records.
- Microphone/speech input and whether audio is processed locally or transmitted.
- Diagnostic, security and server logs.
- Any analytics, crash reporting, hosting, database, AI or other service providers.

Do not assume that a feature is local-only or cloud-connected without testing the released build. Do not collect a child's full name, precise location, phone number, school address, photo or voice recording unless a documented necessity, lawful basis and appropriate parent/guardian process have been established.

## Children and parental involvement
Before enabling child accounts or classroom participation, the publisher must determine which child-privacy laws apply in every launch country and implement the required parent/guardian notice, consent, verification, access and deletion processes. A classroom consent-code workflow is not, by itself, proof of a person's legal guardianship.

## AI and voice features
Before release, disclose whether prompts, transcripts or audio are sent to an AI or speech service, which provider receives them, why they are processed, whether they are retained or used for model improvement, and how a parent can request deletion. Do not enable a live AI voice feature until its backend, safeguards and data handling have been verified.

## Sharing and service providers
List each service provider that receives personal data, its purpose, data categories, processing location and relevant safeguards. Current development uses GitHub Pages and Supabase; the actual production configuration, account data flows and any other processors must be checked before this section is completed. Do not imply that no data is shared until this audit is complete.

## Retention, access and deletion
Specify retention periods for account, classroom, consent, learning-progress, support and security records. Provide a working process for parents/guardians and adult account holders to access, correct or delete eligible data, revoke consent where applicable, and close accounts. Confirm deletion behaviour with backups and third-party processors.

## Security
Describe the safeguards actually implemented in the released system, including authentication, role checks, database Row Level Security, access logging, encryption in transit and incident response. Do not claim a safeguard is present until verified.
