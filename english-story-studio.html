🌱 Story: The Little Seed

Mia found a tiny seed beside the garden path. She placed it in soft soil and gave it a little water.

Each morning, Mia checked the soil. After many sunny days, a small green shoot appeared. Mia smiled and kept caring for the plant.

Soon, a bright yellow flower opened. A bee came to visit. Mia learned that patience and care can help small things grow.

Tip: follow each paragraph with your finger as you listen.

🔤 New vocabulary

seed
the part of a plant that can grow into a new plant
soil
the earth where plants grow
shoot
a new young stem or plant growth
patience
waiting calmly without giving up
