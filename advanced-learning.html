Five advanced learning tools — first prototype

🧭 Your learning dashboard

Your learning record starts on this device. Try a short check-in below.

Your practice history is kept on this device. If you sign in with an approved student account and guardian permission, completed quiz scores can also be saved to your secure learning profile.

Cloud score saving is available after approved student sign-in. Your local record remains available either way.

1 · Learning difficulty detector

🔎 Find what needs more practice

Choose Start check-in to begin a five-question practice.

The questions begin gently and become a little more challenging.

The detector uses answers from this short practice to suggest a next step. It is a learning aid, not a diagnosis or a judgement of ability.

2 · Personal daily learning coach

📅 Your suggested 15-minute plan

Complete a check-in to receive a plan based on your practice.

3 · Multilingual learning

🌐 Learn useful words in another language

Today's phrase

Hello! How are you?

Arabic: مرحباً! كيف حالك؟

This is guided phrase practice with built-in examples. Full multilingual AI teaching and translation quality need the secure AI connection and review by fluent speakers.

4 · Voice-first learning

🎙️ Speak, listen and practise

Voice features depend on your browser, device language and microphone permission.

The microphone converts speech to text in your browser. This prototype does not send recordings or audio to a server.

5 · Real-world learning challenges

🛠️ Learn by solving everyday problems

A school garden has 3 rows. Put 4 seeds in each row. How many seeds will you need?

Try it, explain your thinking, and ask a trusted adult to help you test safe ideas.

🛡️ Safe learning promise

Use a nickname in activities. Never enter your address, phone number, password, school location or private family details. Ask a parent or teacher before using a microphone or doing a practical experiment. Get adult help with tools, cooking, electricity, chemicals or outdoor activities.
