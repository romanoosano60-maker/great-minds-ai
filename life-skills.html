Life Skills & Wellbeing

Learn about healthy habits, feelings, kindness, safety and working together.

← Back to Great Minds Home
A helpful reminder: Everyone learns at a different pace. If something worries you or you feel unsafe, speak to a trusted parent, caregiver, teacher or another safe adult. You do not have to solve a difficult problem alone.
😊

1. Understanding Feelings

Feelings give us clues about what is happening. You might feel happy, worried, excited, angry, calm or sad. All feelings can be talked about safely.

Try: Name the feeling, notice what your body is telling you, and choose a helpful next step such as talking to someone you trust.

💬

2. Kind Communication

Listen without interrupting, use a calm voice, take turns and explain what you need respectfully. It is okay to disagree without being unkind.

Try: Say, “I feel ___ when ___ . Could we ___?”

🧼

3. Healthy Daily Habits

Regular sleep, movement, handwashing, drinking water and a variety of foods help bodies and minds. Ask an adult for help with routines that are difficult.

Try: Make a simple morning or bedtime routine with a grown-up.

🛡️

4. Personal Safety

Learn your trusted adults and how to ask for help. You can say “no” to unwanted touch or an unsafe request. Keep telling a trusted adult until someone helps.

Try: With a caregiver, identify two trusted adults you could talk to if you need help. Do not share their names here.

🔐

5. Digital Safety

Keep passwords private. Do not share your full name, address, school, phone number, live location or private photos online. Ask a trusted adult before clicking unfamiliar links or chatting with someone you do not know.

Try: Practise closing a page and asking an adult when something online feels confusing or uncomfortable.

🧹

6. Responsibility & Routines

Small helpful actions matter: putting things away, caring for books, preparing school items and finishing one task at a time.

Try: Choose one age-appropriate task and make a three-step plan.

🤝

7. Teamwork & Friendship

Include others, share turns, ask before borrowing, apologise when you make a mistake and seek adult help if someone is being hurt or bullied.

Try: Think of one way to help a classmate feel welcome.

🧠

8. Calm Problem-Solving

Pause, describe the problem, think of safe choices, ask for help when needed and try one reasonable step. You can learn from mistakes.

Try: Take a comfortable slow breath, then name one small next step. Stop if breathing exercises feel uncomfortable.
