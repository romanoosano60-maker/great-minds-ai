Reading and Writing

Read a story, listen carefully, answer questions and practise writing.

🏠 HomeLearning Hub

1. Read the story

Mina has a little garden. Every morning, she waters the plants. One sunny day, a yellow butterfly visits the flowers. Mina watches it gently and smiles. She knows plants need sunlight, water and care to grow.

This uses the speech voice available on your phone or browser.

2. Reading comprehension

Question 1: What does Mina water every morning?

Question 2: What visits the flowers?

Score: 0/2

3. Your writing challenge

Write two or three sentences about an animal you like. Describe what it looks like and what it does.

0/1200 characters. Use a nickname only. Do not write private information.
