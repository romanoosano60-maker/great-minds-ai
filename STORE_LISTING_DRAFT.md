## App name
Great Minds AI

## Short description (Google Play draft)
A colourful learning space for children with lessons, stories, quizzes, games and guided discovery.

## Promotional text (Apple draft)
Explore, learn and grow with Great Minds AI — a playful learning space for curious minds.

## Full description (draft)
Great Minds AI brings learning activities together in one child-friendly experience. Children can explore subjects, practise skills and discover new ideas through:

- English learning, vocabulary, spelling, stories and reading practice
- Mathematics, science and home science activities
- Social studies, history and an interactive world-atlas experience
- A child-friendly encyclopedia and guided research activities
- Quizzes, games, stars, badges and learning milestones
- Student, parent and teacher learning areas
- An international classroom experience designed for supervised learning

The app is being developed with parent and teacher involvement in mind. Some features are still prototypes and may require an internet connection or supported browser/device.

## Decisions required from the owner
- Confirm intended age range. Current working direction is children around 9–12, but the exact range must be confirmed before store declarations.
- Confirm launch countries and languages.
- Confirm whether the first release is free, paid, subscription-based or free with optional adult-managed purchases. No price is assumed.
- Confirm whether ads will be absent. For a child-focused product, an ad-free first release is the safer and simpler starting point, subject to owner approval.
- Supply a monitored support contact and legal business/publisher details when ready.

## Important submission boundary
The current project is a GitHub Pages web application. It is not yet a signed Android App Bundle or iOS archive. A native packaging approach (or a properly designed web-app distribution route where supported) and real-device release testing are required before store submission. Do not claim that the app is already available in Google Play or the Apple App Store.
