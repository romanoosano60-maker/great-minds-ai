# Great Minds AI — Play Store and App Appearance

## Approved brand direction

**Great Minds AI**  
**Where Young Minds Grow!**  
Tagline: **Learn • Explore • Achieve • Grow**

Core message:
**CHILDREN DISCOVER. PARENTS EMPOWER. TEACHERS INSPIRE.**

Positioning:
One welcoming learning community that brings children, parents, and teachers together.

## Three audience promises

### For children — A world of fun and discovery
Learning should feel like an adventure. Children explore subjects, read, practise, solve challenges, play educational games, ask questions, and celebrate progress.

### For parents — Confidence in their child's learning journey
Help parents support learning at home, encourage good habits, follow available learning progress, support homework, and celebrate milestones. Only describe progress features as available after end-to-end testing.

### For teachers — A classroom full of possibilities
Support classroom activities, participation, learning practice, homework, and student development. Only describe classroom collaboration and reporting as live after end-to-end testing.

## Recommended visual identity

- Keep the existing colourful rocket-themed Great Minds AI home screen as the single main app entry point.
- Use a bright, welcoming, child-friendly 3D illustration style.
- Main visual: a friendly rocket journey through a colourful learning universe, with diverse children exploring books, science, maths, nature, and technology.
- Supporting scenes: a parent encouraging a child at home; a friendly teacher guiding a diverse classroom; children learning together around a globe.
- Use a consistent palette of deep navy/space blue with bright, joyful accents such as teal, green, yellow, orange, and purple.
- Keep text large, readable, and uncluttered on Android screens.
- Show real app screenshots in Play Store screenshots; do not present concept illustrations as actual app functionality.

## Accuracy and child-safety guardrails

- Do not claim that unrestricted live AI conversations are available while the AI Teacher endpoint is unconfigured.
- Do not claim that all lessons are fully translated into every listed language; translation coverage and device voice availability need testing.
- Do not claim live international classrooms are operational until real cross-device and cross-country testing passes.
- Do not imply parent/teacher dashboards, cloud progress, or homework sync are fully reliable until real account workflows pass.
- Use genuine screenshots for app features and original licensed artwork for promotional illustrations.
- Complete privacy, parental-consent, child-safety, and Google Play policy reviews before publication.
