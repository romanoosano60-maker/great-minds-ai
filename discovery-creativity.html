Discovery & Creativity Centre

Imagine, investigate, build, create and explore through eight playful learning stations.

← Back to Great Minds Home
Learn safely: Ask a parent or teacher before using tools, materials, going outdoors or trying an experiment. Never enter your full name, address, school, phone number, passwords or other private information.
💡

1. Young Inventors Lab

Find everyday problems and imagine helpful inventions. Plan, test and improve your ideas.

Inventor challenge

How could you keep a favourite book dry on a rainy day?

Draw your idea on paper and explain how it works.

🪐

2. Space Explorer

Discover planets, stars, the Moon and our Solar System.

Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
Choose an answer.
🐾

3. Animal Kingdom

Explore animal groups, habitats, food, movement and care for living things.

Is a dolphin a fish or a mammal?
Choose a group.
🎨

4. Creative Art Studio

Create original pictures, patterns, stories and designs. There is no single right answer in art.

Art idea

Design a friendly robot that helps plants grow.

Use paper, crayons or a drawing app. Give your artwork a title.

🪙

5. Young Entrepreneurs

Learn how people solve problems, plan an idea and make thoughtful choices.

Mini market maths

You have 10 play coins. A bookmark costs 3 and a pencil costs 2. How many remain after buying one of each?

Use pretend coins only. No real purchases.
🌍

6. World Explorer

Learn about continents, oceans, languages, landmarks and cultures.

Which is the largest ocean on Earth?
Choose an ocean.

Find it on a map or globe with an adult.

🧪

7. Science Experiment Centre

Ask questions, make predictions, observe carefully and describe results.

Shadow observation

Watch a shadow change during the day from a safe place. Never look directly at the Sun.

This activity needs only observation and adult guidance.
🧩

8. Puzzle & Logic Centre

Build reasoning with patterns, number puzzles, memory and step-by-step thinking.

What number comes next? 2, 4, 6, 8, …
Choose the next number.
For parents and teachers: Encourage children to explain their thinking, try different solutions and celebrate effort. Activities run in the browser and do not submit personal details or create cloud progress records.
