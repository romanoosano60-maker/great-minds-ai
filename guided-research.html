📝 My discovery notes

Research habit: use trusted books, lessons and adult-approved sources. Check who created information and when. Never share your full name, address, passwords or private photos.

🕵️ Is this source trustworthy?

Use this checklist with a book, lesson, website or other adult-approved source. A checklist helps you think; it cannot prove that every claim is true.

1. Who made it?

Look for an author, teacher, school, museum or organisation. If nobody is named, ask an adult to help check it.

2. When was it made or updated?

Check the date. Some facts change, especially information about technology, health and current events.

3. What evidence is given?

Look for explanations, examples, observations or references—not just a strong opinion or a surprising headline.

4. Can another trusted source confirm it?

Compare the key fact with a second independent book, lesson or adult-approved source. If they disagree, pause and ask a teacher.
